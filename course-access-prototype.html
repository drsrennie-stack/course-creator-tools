How navigation works

Step 1. Pick your course at the top (Anatomy, A&P, Physiology).
Step 2. The hub page shows the course toolkit (admin, study tools, practice, projects) and a grid of modules. Click a module.
Step 3. The module page shows its topics. Each topic has colored circles: R (required), H (highly recommended), O (optional), and A (anatomy refresher, Physiology only). Click a circle to see those resources for that topic.
